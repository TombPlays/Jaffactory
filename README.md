# Jaffactory
Adds the ability to manufacture Chocolatey, and Orangey, Biscuit-Shaped Cakes. Other baked goods are available, but aren't as good.

## Quick Thank You
### to everyone who enjoyed this mod. This was my first mod for Minecraft. no AI no automatic tooling. Just a first foray into Java.
### It was always meant as a short term solution until the official modpack and mods were released. I didn't expect this mod's life to be measured in years.
